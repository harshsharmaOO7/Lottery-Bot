❓ Frequently Asked Questions

What time is Lottery Sambad result published?
Results are published three times daily: 1:00 PM, 6:00 PM, and 8:00 PM IST. This page auto-updates within minutes of each draw via our automated bot.
How to download Nagaland Lottery Result PDF?
Click the "Download PDF" button above after the draw time. The official government PDF opens and can be saved to your device. You can also view it directly in the browser PDF viewer on this page.
Is Nagaland State Lottery legal in India?
Yes. Nagaland State Lottery is 100% legal, regulated by the Government of Nagaland since 1972 under the Lotteries (Regulation) Act, 1998.
How long to claim lottery prize money?
After submitting all required documents with the original winning ticket, prize money is processed within 3 working days. Sign the back of your ticket immediately.

Lottery Sambad Result Today — Official Source

Lottery Sambad is India's most popular state lottery, operated by the Government of Nagaland. Results are published three times every day — at 1:00 PM, 6:00 PM, and 8:00 PM IST. This page is automatically updated within minutes of each draw using official government PDFs.

Nagaland State Lottery Draws

Each draw has a unique name based on the day of the week. The 1PM draw is the "Dear Morning" series, 6PM is the "Dear Evening" series, and 8PM is the "Dear Night" series. First prize is ₹1 Crore.

Kerala Lottery Result Today

Kerala Lottery results are published daily at 3:00 PM IST. The seven weekly draws include Win-Win, Akshaya, Karunya Plus, Nirmal, and Pournami — all operated by the Government of Kerala since 1967.
