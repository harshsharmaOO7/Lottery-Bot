Kerala State Lottery Result Today

Kerala State Lottery has been operated by the Government of Kerala since 1967. Seven weekly draws: Monday Win-Win, Tuesday Sthree Sakthi, Wednesday Akshaya, Thursday Karunya Plus, Friday Nirmal, Saturday Karunya, Sunday Pournami. Results at 3PM IST.
